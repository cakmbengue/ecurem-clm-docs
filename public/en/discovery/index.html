Discovery

Discover TLS services and certificates within an authorized scope while keeping control of the scan boundary.

On this pageExternal discoveryLocal discoveryTargets and scopeResultsGood practices

External discovery

External discovery observes Internet-reachable services within the defined scope and helps identify forgotten or expiring certificates.

Local discovery

For internal networks, use the Discovery Agent to execute scans from an environment authorized by your organization.

Targets and scope

Start with a small number of targets and expand gradually. Never include systems for which you do not have explicit authorization.

Results

Review findings before import or automation: results may contain test services, duplicates or systems outside the team’s responsibility.

Good practices

  • document scope;
  • coordinate internal scans;
  • avoid broad first scans;
  • validate findings before bulk action.
