DISCOVERY

Discovery

Understand certificate discovery and prepare a controlled scan scope.

Purpose

Discovery helps identify certificates and TLS services within an authorized scope and bring useful findings into the inventory.

External and local discovery

External scans observe services reachable from the Internet. The Discovery Agent extends discovery to internal networks authorized by the organization.

Discovery Agent

The Agent must be enrolled into the expected organization before use. Scan scope must be limited to networks and systems the customer is authorized to assess.

Scope and ports

Targets, ports and ranges depend on the Discovery configuration. Coordinate local scan scope with network and security teams before scanning.

Domain validation

Domain validation confirms that a domain can be used for operations requiring proof of control. Complete validation before enabling sensitive automation.

Good practices

  • Start with a small scope.
  • Avoid unauthorized ranges.
  • Review findings before bulk import.
  • Document exclusions and out-of-scope systems.
