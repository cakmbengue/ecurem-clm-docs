Tags & targeting

Use tags to group and target assets without mixing organizational scopes.

On this pageWhy use tagsOperational targetingIsolationNaming convention

Why use tags

Tags organize domains and certificates according to your model: environment, team, application, criticality or renewal group.

Operational targeting

Use tags to restrict bulk operations to intended objects and review the selection before launch.

Isolation

Tags are interpreted within the organization context. Two organizations may use similar names without creating a shared scope.

Naming convention

Use a simple documented convention such as prod, preprod, web or renewal-auto.
