Domain validation

Confirm control of a domain before operations that require proof of control.

On this pageWhy validateBefore validationBulk validationControl changes

Why validate

Validation reduces the risk of performing sensitive operations on a domain the organization does not control or no longer controls.

Before validation

Confirm the domain owner, operational contacts and DNS provider. Keep technical credentials limited to the required scope.

Bulk validation

For multiple domains, select only domains whose control has been established. Bulk action does not replace scope verification.

Control changes

Validation may need to be repeated when domain control changes or when policy requires revalidation.
