Security & trust

Public overview of the security principles used by Ecurem CLM SaaS.

Organization isolation

Ecurem CLM is designed as a multi-tenant platform. Data and operations are associated with an organization so users from one organization cannot operate on another organization’s objects.

Authentication and 2FA

User access relies on platform authentication and two-factor authentication. Administrative and sensitive operations remain limited to authorized profiles.

Data and secrets

Technical secrets used by integrations should not be shared unnecessarily. Customers remain responsible for credentials they provide for DNS providers, certificate authorities and infrastructure.

Logging and traceability

Important technical actions should remain traceable to support operations, incident analysis and internal controls.

Shared responsibility

Ecurem secures and operates the SaaS platform. Customers remain responsible for the scan scope they authorize, the credentials they provide and the automation policies they enable.
