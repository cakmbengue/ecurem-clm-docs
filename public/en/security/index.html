Security & trust

Public security and shared-responsibility principles for Ecurem CLM SaaS.

On this pageOrganization isolationAuthentication and 2FALeast privilegeSecrets and integrationsTraceabilityShared responsibility

Organization isolation

Ecurem CLM is designed to separate data and operations by organization. Users work within their organization context and administrative functions are governed by granted permissions.

Authentication and 2FA

Two-factor authentication is part of the account protection model. Administrative and privileged accounts deserve additional care.

Least privilege

Grant only the permissions required for each role. Access to sensitive operations, integrations and settings should remain limited to authorized profiles.

Secrets and integrations

DNS-provider credentials, certificate-authority credentials, API tokens and other integration secrets should be treated as sensitive. Limit their scope and revoke them when no longer needed.

Traceability

Important operations should be attributable to a user, job or event to support operations and incident analysis.

Shared responsibility

Ecurem protects and operates the SaaS platform. Customers remain responsible for authorized users, scan scope, supplied secrets and enabled automation policies.
