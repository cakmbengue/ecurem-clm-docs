AI Assistant

Use the assistant as an operational aid without delegating governance to it.

On this pageRoleOrganizational dataHuman validationGood use

Role

The assistant helps users understand inventory, expirations, Discovery, Renewal and other information available within the user’s authorized context.

Organizational data

Use the assistant within your organization context and avoid entering unnecessary secrets or credentials.

Human validation

Responses are guidance. Sensitive actions, security decisions and operational changes should be validated by an authorized person.

Good use

  • ask a precise question;
  • verify referenced objects and dates;
  • use platform screens as the operational source;
  • never paste a password, private key or token.
