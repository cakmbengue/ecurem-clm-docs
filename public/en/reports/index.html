REPORTS

Reports & compliance

Turn cryptographic inventory data into assessments, findings, evidence and exports for security reviews.

On this page1. Dashboard2. Scopes3. Assessment4. Metrics5. Findings6. Exports7. Interpretation

1. Dashboard

Reports opens the cryptographic compliance and readiness dashboard for the active organization, with actions to manage scopes and launch assessments.

2. Scopes

A scope defines which assets are included. Keep production, pre-production and demonstration assets separated where different conclusions are required.

3. Assessment

An assessment creates a campaign against the selected scope. Open the campaign to review controls, evidence, findings and remediation actions.

4. Metrics

Dashboard metrics can include assessable certificates, expired certificates, weak algorithms, untrusted chains, post-quantum visibility and open actions.

5. Findings

Review control status, summary, assurance level and evidence. Missing evidence may produce a not-assessed result rather than a compliant result.

6. Exports

  • Campaign CSV
  • Evidence CSV
  • PDF report

7. Interpretation

Missing evidence does not mean compliance. Confirm organization, scope, collection date and included assets before sharing a report.
