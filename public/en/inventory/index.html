Inventory

Use the inventory as the organization’s visibility layer for tracked certificates.

On this pagePurposePopulationData qualityOperational use

Purpose

The inventory centralizes known certificates and key attributes such as issuer, validity dates, domains, algorithms and operational status.

Population

The inventory can be enriched through Discovery, authorized imports or lifecycle operations performed in the platform.

Data quality

Before automating an action, confirm that the certificate and domain correspond to the actual service in use.

Operational use

Use the inventory to prioritize expirations, identify anomalies, prepare renewals and document decisions.
