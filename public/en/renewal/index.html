RENEWAL

Renewal

Automate certificate renewal without losing control of validation, issuance and deployment.

On this pagePrepare the renewalIssuanceDeploymentPost-deployment verificationFailure

Prepare the renewal

Confirm the domain, validation method, expected authority or provider, and deployment target.

Issuance

Use the method approved by the organization and limit required secrets to the operation scope.

Deployment

A renewed certificate is useful only if it is deployed to the correct target. Verify the certificate actually served after the operation.

Post-deployment verification

Check the domain, issuer, new expiration date and chain presented by the target service.

Failure

Avoid repeated retries without diagnosis. Check domain validation, CA or DNS access, target configuration and the job log.
