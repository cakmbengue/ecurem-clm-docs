RENEWAL

Renewal

Prepare and control certificate renewal automation.

Before automating

Confirm that the domain is under your control, the validation method works and the deployment target is correctly identified.

Renewal policies

A policy defines how and under which conditions a certificate should be renewed. Do not apply automation policies to certificates retained only for demonstration or monitoring.

Tags and targeting

Tags can group domains or certificates that should receive the same treatment and restrict renewal operations to an explicit scope.

Validation and deployment

After issuance or renewal, verify that the expected certificate is actually deployed on the target service with the expected chain and expiration date.

If a renewal fails

  • Check domain validation.
  • Check DNS-provider or CA access.
  • Review the job log.
  • Avoid repeated retries until the cause is understood.
