Network & firewall

Prepare connectivity required for SaaS access, Discovery and integrations.

On this pageUser accessDiscovery AgentLocal scansIntegrationsLeast exposure

User access

Users access Ecurem CLM over HTTPS. Workstations must resolve the service name and establish the TLS connection.

Discovery Agent

The Agent must establish outbound HTTPS connectivity to Ecurem CLM and reach the configured internal targets.

Local scans

Ports between the Agent and targets depend on services being discovered. Open only the flows required for approved scope.

Integrations

DNS, CA, email or other external integrations may require additional outbound connectivity depending on the provider.

Least exposure

Do not open inbound ports to the Agent solely for SaaS operation unless your architecture requires them.
