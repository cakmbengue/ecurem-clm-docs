Renewal policies

Control automation with explicit rules and a well-defined scope.

On this pagePurpose of a policyScopeChangesControl

Purpose of a policy

A policy defines when a certificate may be renewed and which method is used.

Scope

Apply a policy only to certificates and domains that should actually be automated. Demonstration certificates should remain outside automation.

Changes

Treat policy changes as operational changes: review, test on a limited scope, then expand progressively.

Control

After activation, monitor jobs, errors and deployment results.
