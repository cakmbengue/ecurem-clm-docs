FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Operational answers to common Ecurem CLM questions.

On this pageInventoryDiscoveryAgentRenewalPKIReportsAI AssistantSecurity

Inventory

Is every discovered certificate automatically managed?

No. Discovery provides visibility. Use Inventory, tags and internal governance to decide what should enter Renewal.

Discovery

Can I scan any domain or network?

No. The scope must be authorized by your organization.

Why are no certificates found?

Check DNS, routing, firewall, target port and actual TLS service availability.

Discovery Agent

Do I need inbound Internet access to the Agent?

No for normal SaaS communication. The Agent uses outbound HTTPS and must reach internal targets on configured ports.

Renewal

Are issuance and deployment the same?

No. A certificate can be issued but not yet served by the target application.

Client PKI

Can Ecurem CLM work with an offline root?

Yes. An offline root can be referenced/imported while operational authorities handle day-to-day issuance.

Reports

Does “Not assessed” mean compliant?

No. It means available evidence is insufficient to conclude.

AI Assistant

Can Aida modify certificates?

The tools documented here are read-only and operate within the active organization.

Security

Is 2FA mandatory?

Yes.

Where is the Ecurem ACM guide?

It remains at ecurem.cloud/en/guide.
