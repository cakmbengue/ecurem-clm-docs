Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common Ecurem CLM questions.

Does Ecurem CLM replace my certificate authority?

Not necessarily. Ecurem CLM orchestrates and governs certificate lifecycle operations and can integrate with certificate authorities or providers according to your architecture.

Can I discover internal certificates?

Yes, when the scope is authorized and a Discovery Agent is used within the appropriate network.

Can I perform bulk actions?

Selection and targeting capabilities support grouped operations. Always review scope before confirming.

Should I send a private key to support?

No. Never send a private key, password or token in a support request.

Where is the Ecurem ACM guide?

The Ecurem ACM guide remains available at ecurem.cloud/en/guide. This documentation is dedicated to Ecurem CLM SaaS.
